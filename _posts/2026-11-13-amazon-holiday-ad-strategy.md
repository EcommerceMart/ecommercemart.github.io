            const adSpend = parseFloat(document.getElementById('adSpend').value);
            const salesGenerated = parseFloat(document.getElementById('salesGenerated').value);
            const cogsPercentage = parseFloat(document.getElementById('cogsPercentage').value) / 100;

                return;

            // Calculate COGS and Amazon Fees in absolute terms
            const cogs = salesGenerated * cogsPercentage;
            const amazonFees = salesGenerated * amazonFeesPercentage;

            // Calculate Gross Profit from Sales
            const grossProfitFromSales = salesGenerated - cogs - amazonFees;

            // Calculate Net Profit from Ads
            const netProfitFromAds = grossProfitFromSales - adSpend;

            // Calculate Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

Not all ad placements on Amazon are created equal. ### Proactive Fraud Detection and Click Anomaly Analysis

Ad fraud, including invalid clicks from bots or malicious competitors, can quickly drain your Amazon holiday ad spend. AI systems are adept at monitoring for suspicious patterns and click anomalies that indicate fraudulent activity. They can identify unusual click rates, geographic anomalies, or repeated clicks from the same IP address.

These sophisticated algorithms work in real-time to flag and mitigate such threats, protecting your budget from being siphoned off by non-human or malicious traffic. This level of protection is challenging to achieve with manual oversight. AI provides an essential layer of security for your valuable ad budget.

By proactively detecting and preventing ad fraud, AI ensures that your advertising investment is spent on genuine potential customers. This not only reduces costs but also provides more accurate performance data for future campaign optimization. It's a crucial aspect of responsible budget optimization Amazon.
